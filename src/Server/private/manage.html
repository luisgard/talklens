Add your guest list

A

If you use Luma

Open your event in Luma, go to its guests, and download the list as a CSV file. Then upload that file here.

B

If you don't

Download our template, open it in Excel or Google Sheets, write one person per row with their name and email, and save it as CSV.

At the door, your team finds each person by name or email, so nobody needs a QR code. Uploading the same list again only adds who is new.

Privacy and access optional

The defaults are safe. Change these only if you need to.

When someone accepts a request to meet, they pick one of these. Leave it empty and phones offer the entrance, the coffee area and outside the room.

People outside the venue (technical)

Off by default. It needs a tunnel you run yourself, such as: cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:8080
